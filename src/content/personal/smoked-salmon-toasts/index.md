**Serves:** 1–2 · **Time:** 10 min

## Ingredients

- 2 slices sourdough bread
- Trader Joe's British hardwood smoked salmon
- 1 egg
- Avocado
- Cucumber, sliced
- Sesame seeds
- Furikake
- Black pepper

## Method

1. I boil the egg for 7 minutes for a jammy yolk, then cool it in cold water and peel it.
2. While the egg cooks, I toast the bread.
3. I mash the avocado onto one slice and layer cucumber slices on the other.
4. I add the smoked salmon to both, then half an egg on each.
5. I finish with black pepper, sesame seeds on the avocado toast and furikake on the cucumber toast.
