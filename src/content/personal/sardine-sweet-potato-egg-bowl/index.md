**Serves:** 1 · **Time:** 40 min

## Ingredients

- Sweet potato, diced
- Olive oil
- Italian seasoning
- 1 tin sardines
- 1 egg
- Mixed greens
- Balsamic vinegar
- Honey
- Chilli flakes

## Method

1. I toss the diced sweet potato in olive oil and Italian seasoning, then roast it in the oven for 30 minutes until golden.
2. I fry the egg.
3. I arrange the mixed greens, sweet potato and sardines straight from the tin in a bowl, then add the fried egg.
4. I mix balsamic vinegar, honey and chilli flakes, and drizzle it over the egg and sardines.
