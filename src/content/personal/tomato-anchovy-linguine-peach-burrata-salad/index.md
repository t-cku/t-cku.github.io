**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 30 min

## Ingredients

- Linguine
- 1 box cherry tomatoes
- 2 anchovies
- Garlic
- Tomato paste
- Olive oil
- Peaches, sliced
- Butter
- Lettuce
- Cured meats
- Burrata
- Balsamic

## Method

1. I cook the linguine in salted water.
2. Meanwhile, I cook the garlic and anchovies in olive oil, then add the cherry tomatoes and a little tomato paste and let the tomatoes burst into a sauce.
3. I add the linguine to the sauce with some pasta water and toss it through.
4. I caramelise the peach slices in butter.
5. I arrange the lettuce on a plate, then add the peaches, cured meats and burrata, and finish with a drizzle of balsamic.
